# UAE Golden Visa Readiness Pack

Objective: position Rafael Rodrigues for the most realistic UAE Golden Visa route based on his current background: senior BI, analytics engineering, data visualization, cloud analytics, and technology specialist experience.

## Best Route

The strongest route is **Skilled Professional / Specialist in Information Technology or Business & Management**, not Scientist/Researcher.

Why:

- The skilled professional route fits senior BI, analytics, IT, technology, business intelligence, and cloud analytics roles.
- The scientist/researcher route usually requires research indicators such as H-index, FWCI, Scopus profile, publications, or official recommendation.
- The skilled professional route is more realistic if a UAE employer hires Rafael at or above the salary threshold.

## Target Job Titles For Golden Visa Alignment

Use titles that look like professional specialist roles, not junior analyst roles.

| Best Title | Why It Helps |

| Senior BI Consultant | Professional consulting role, not junior analyst |
| Analytics Engineer | Strong IT/data classification fit |
| Senior Analytics Engineer | Better for salary threshold |
| BI Solution Consultant | Specialist role with client/business impact |
| Power BI Consultant | Direct fit with certification and market demand |
| Data Visualization Consultant | Specialist role in BI/analytics |
| Business Intelligence Consultant | Clear professional category |
| Executive Reporting Consultant | Business/management + analytics |
| Cloud Analytics Consultant | Fits technology and cloud |

Avoid using only:

- Data Analyst
- Reporting Analyst
- Dashboard Developer

These can pay below the threshold and may look less senior.
