# Interview Explanation Guide

## Very Short Version

I built a BI portfolio with public company data.

It shows dashboards, KPIs, business insights, and simple decision scores.

My goal is to show how I help companies and governments make better decisions with trusted data.

## Explain It Like A Child

Imagine each company has a school report card.

I give a grade for four things:

- how big the company is;
- how much profit it keeps;
- how much it invests in technology;
- how much value the team creates.

Then I put the grades together into one score.

The best score goes to the top.

This helps leaders see who is strong, who is efficient, and what needs attention.

## Technical Version

The dashboard uses official public data.

I calculate:

- revenue;
- net income;
- net margin;
- technology investment intensity;
- revenue per employee;
- decision score.

The decision score uses clear weights:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Revenue scale | 30% |
| Net margin | 30% |
| Technology intensity | 20% |
| Productivity | 20% |

The formula is not hidden. It is clear and easy to change.

## Why This Is Useful

Most companies already have data.

The hard part is to make the data trusted, simple, and useful for decisions.

This project shows that I can:

- define KPIs;
- clean and organize metrics;
- build executive dashboards;
- explain data in business language;
- create simple decision models;
- work with Power BI, Tableau, Looker, BigQuery, Snowflake, AWS, SQL, Python, and dbt.

## My Best Interview Sentence

I start with the business question, define the right KPIs, build the trusted model, and make the decision easy for leadership.
