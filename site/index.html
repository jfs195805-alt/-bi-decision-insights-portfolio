    Executive Decision Engine

    This is a simple score that turns business numbers into a ranked list. Each company gets points for size, profit, technology investment, and productivity.

    Explain It Simply

    If this were a school report card, every company gets four grades.

    Revenue shows how big the company is. Margin shows how much profit stays in the company. Technology intensity shows how much the company invests in the future. Productivity shows how much value the team creates. The dashboard puts these grades together into one score.

    Score = Scale 30% + Margin 30% + Tech 20% + Productivity 20%
